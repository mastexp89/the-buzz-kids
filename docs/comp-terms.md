# Buzz comp T&Cs — reusable blocks

Fill the [placeholders], keep the Facebook disclaimer word-for-word. Works for The Buzz Guide and Buzz Kids — swap the promoter/page name. (Not legal advice — if a comp ever involves big-ticket prizes or paid entry, run it past a solicitor.)

---

## Short block — paste at the bottom of every comp post

> **HOW TO ENTER:** Like this post and comment [your answer / tag optional] by [closing date, time]. One entry per person. Winner drawn at random and announced on this page. Feel free to tag a mate and share — lovely, but not required to enter. 18+, UK residents. Full terms: [link to terms page]. This promotion is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Facebook/Meta.

## Full terms — host once on thebuzzguide.co.uk / thebuzzkids.co.uk and link to it

**[Page name] Competition Terms & Conditions**

1. **Promoter:** [The Buzz Guide / Buzz Kids], [trading name / address, e.g. Forth Host & Web Design, Dundee].
2. **Eligibility:** Open to UK residents aged 18 or over (or [16+] where the prize is suitable — state per comp). Employees of the promoter and their immediate families may not enter.
3. **How to enter:** Like the competition post and leave a comment on it before the closing date stated in the post. No purchase necessary. Internet access and a Facebook account are required. One entry per person; duplicate comments count as a single entry.
4. **Optional actions:** Entrants may be invited to tag friends, share the post, or follow the page. These are optional and do not affect the chance of winning.
5. **Closing date:** As stated in the competition post. Entries after the closing date will not be counted.
6. **Winner selection:** One winner (unless stated otherwise) will be selected at random from all valid entries using an independent random draw tool. The draw reference is retained so the result can be independently reproduced.
7. **Notification:** The winner will be announced on this page and/or contacted via Facebook within [3] days of the draw. If the winner does not respond within [7] days of notification, the promoter reserves the right to redraw.
8. **Prize:** As described in the competition post. No cash alternative. The prize is non-transferable. The promoter reserves the right to substitute a prize of equal or greater value if circumstances require.
9. **Data:** Entrants' public Facebook names are used solely to administer the draw and announce the winner, and are not used for marketing or shared with third parties.
10. **General:** The promoter's decision is final. By entering, entrants agree to these terms. The promoter reserves the right to cancel or amend the competition if circumstances outside its control make this necessary.
11. **Facebook:** This promotion is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Facebook/Meta. Each entrant completely releases Facebook/Meta from any liability in connection with this promotion. Information is provided to the promoter, not to Facebook.

## Why the mechanics are worded this way

- "Comment + like" are the only entry actions Facebook's promotion policy permits pages to **require** — and they're exactly what the draw tool verifies automatically.
- "Tag / share / follow" must be optional: Facebook's policy prohibits using personal timelines and friend connections to administer promotions ("share to enter" / "tag your friends to enter" are named as not permitted). Encouraging them is fine.
- Announcing the draw ref (from the tool) alongside the winner supports the "verifiably fair random draw" standard UK prize promotions are expected to meet (CAP Code).
